Meet your dermatologist

Dr Tay Liang Kiat

Dr Tay practises medical, surgical and cosmetic dermatology, with particular expertise in skin cancer, Mohs micrographic surgery, dermatologic and nail surgery.

His clinical care also includes adult and children’s skin conditions, including eczema, psoriasis, acne and urticaria.

Which clinic can I request?

The official contact page lists Parkway East Medical Centre at 319 Joo Chiat Place, Suite #04-04, and Shaw Centre at 1 Scotts Road, #03-02. Tell the team which location you prefer and confirm availability before visiting.

Does Dr Tay see adults and children?

Yes. Dr Tay manages adult and children’s skin conditions, alongside skin cancer, surgical and cosmetic dermatology.

Can I enquire about Mohs surgery?

Yes. Dr Tay has fellowship training in Mohs micrographic surgery at Oxford. A consultation is needed to discuss your condition and whether this or another treatment is appropriate.

Can I ask about insurance coverage?

Yes. The clinic lists insurance partners on its website. Eligibility depends on your plan, procedure and any policy requirements. Ask the clinic and your insurer to confirm coverage before treatment.

Is my appointment confirmed when I enquire?

No. Please wait for the clinic team to confirm the date, time and location. You can call 6539 9365 or WhatsApp 9159 2824 for assistance.
