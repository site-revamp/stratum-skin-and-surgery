# Stratum Skin and Surgery — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Stratum Skin and Surgery is a Singapore dermatology practice led by Dr Tay Liang Kiat, providing medical, surgical and cosmetic care for adults and children. Its expertise includes skin cancer assessment, Mohs micrographic surgery and dermatologic surgery, with locations at Parkway East and Shaw Centre.

The redesigned homepage simplifies treatment discovery, clarifies appointment enquiry options and helps patients specify their preferred clinic.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. Simpler Treatment Navigation

A simplified main menu and six clear care pathways link directly to relevant pages. Previously, visitors had to work through multiple navigation levels. The revised structure provides clearer starting points and more direct access to care information.

### 2. Direct Appointment Enquiry Options

A dedicated appointment section provides Call and WhatsApp buttons alongside a clearly labelled online enquiry form link. Previously, “Book an Appointment” opened a general contact page, requiring visitors to find their preferred contact method. The new section makes these choices explicit.

### 3. Location-Specific Clinic Enquiries

Separate clinic cards include addresses, maps and WhatsApp messages prefilled with the selected location. Previously, Parkway East and Shaw Centre shared contact details without location-specific enquiry options. Patients can now indicate their preferred clinic when starting a conversation.

## Design Improvements

- **Navigation and hierarchy:** A simplified menu and six pathways organise treatment choices.
- **Action clarity:** The appointment section distinguishes calls, WhatsApp and online enquiries.
- **Consistency:** Clinic cards present addresses, maps and enquiry actions in a consistent format.
- **User experience:** Direct links and location-prefilled messages reduce the steps needed to explore care and enquire.
